# Day 2 — Tuesday 22 September (Morning): Neutron and X-ray Sources & Detectors

## Topic

Modelling of neutron and X-ray sources, and simulation of detectors.

## Content

- How neutron and X-ray sources are characterised and modelled in McStas/McXtrace
- Detector simulation: types, response functions, and noise models
- Understanding how sources and detectors influence the quality of experimental data
- Integrating source and detector components into virtual setups to generate realistic datasets

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this session, participants should be able to:

- Set up a basic virtual source in McStas or McXtrace
- Configure a detector component and interpret its output
- Understand the impact of source brightness and detector resolution on simulated data quality
